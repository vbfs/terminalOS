What you get

The workspace AI-native development demands.

You already moved to AI-first development. Your tools should have too. terminalOS is the first terminal designed around how you actually work — not how developers worked in 1988.

01
Every window, one place
Split your workspace however you need — AI session on the left, test runner on the right, docs below. All in a single window. Your layout is saved automatically and restored exactly on every launch. No more hunting across a dozen tabs.
02
Know what your AI is spending
Token counts and real USD costs surface in your status bar as you work — per session and across your entire workspace. No API key required. No third-party service. No bill shock at the end of the month.
03
Files and docs without leaving the terminal
A built-in editor with live markdown preview, syntax highlighting for 25+ languages, and a full file browser — all inside the same window. Review AI-generated docs, edit prompts, rename files, without touching another app.
04
Always know where you are
Your current folder, git branch, conda environment, and working directory are always visible in the status bar. Ten recent workspaces a keystroke away. Context switching ends when you know your context at a glance.
05
Launch AI tools in one keystroke
Open Claude Code, OpenCode, or Aider from the command palette without typing a command. terminalOS detects when AI is running and shows a live status — so you always know if it's still thinking or waiting for you.
06
Errors you catch before they cost you
Invalid API keys, auth conflicts, and model errors are surfaced as visual alerts the moment they happen — not discovered after 10 minutes of a silent session that was failing the entire time.
